What Are Reward Points?

Reward points are earned through credit cards, shopping, and loyalty programs.

credit_card

Credit Cards

Travel rewards credit cards are one of the fastest ways to earn points. Many cards offer welcome bonuses and earn points on everyday purchases like groceries, gas, and dining.

shopping_bag

Shopping Rewards

Online shopping portals allow you to earn extra points when purchasing from stores you already use. These rewards can stack with credit card earnings to increase your total points.

flight

Loyalty Programs

Airline, hotel, and store loyalty programs help you earn points over time. Many are free to join and can lead to future travel rewards, discounts, or member perks.

Beginner Tip

💡 Did You Know?

Many travel credit cards allow points to be redeemed through their travel portal. While this option is convenient, it may not always provide the best value. In some cases, transferring points to airline or hotel partners can stretch your points further and reduce the number of points needed for the same trip.
