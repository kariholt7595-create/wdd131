Using Points for Flights

Learn how reward points can be redeemed for airline tickets and how to compare different booking options.

Why Use Points for Flights?

Flights are one of the most popular ways to use reward points. Depending on the airline and booking method, points can help reduce the cost of travel and make bigger trips feel more affordable.

travel_explore

Travel Portals

Credit card travel portals are convenient because you can search and book flights in one place using points or cash.

flight

Airline Programs

Airline loyalty programs let travelers earn and redeem miles directly with airlines for future flights.

sync_alt

Transfer Partners

Some credit card points can be transferred to airline partners, which may provide better value than booking through a portal.

Flight Tip

💡 Compare Before You Book

Before using points for a flight, compare the credit card portal price with the airline's own rewards program. Sometimes the same flight can cost fewer points when booked through an airline or transfer partner.
