Using Points for Hotels

Learn how hotel points can help reduce lodging costs and make travel more affordable.

Why Use Points for Hotels?

Hotel stays can be one of the most expensive parts of a trip. Hotel points can help cover free nights, reduce out-of-pocket costs, and sometimes unlock extra perks during your stay.

hotel

Free Nights

Many hotel programs allow members to redeem points for free nights, which can make family trips or longer stays more affordable.

workspace_premium

Hotel Loyalty

Joining hotel loyalty programs is usually free and can help travelers earn points, receive member rates, and access special perks.

upgrade

Upgrades & Perks

Some programs offer extra benefits like room upgrades, late checkout, or free breakfast depending on the hotel brand and membership level.

Hotel Tip

💡 Did You Know?

Hotel points are not always the best redemption option. Depending on the hotel and the cost of the stay, a points eraser feature may require fewer points than booking directly through a hotel rewards program.
